01 Studio Philosophy Akosombo, Ghana · Worldwide

Moments Worth Saving Deserve More Than a Status Post.

Jephthah Kwame Lanor Founder, Designer & Developer #thechucksdeveloper

Social stories vanish after 24 hours. A WhatsApp greeting is buried beneath incoming notifications by morning. We believe your most meaningful milestones deserve their own permanent, art-directed space.

Cupid Studio designs private, bespoke digital keepsakes and invitations. Every commission is coded individually with interactive wax-sealed letters, synchronized soundtrack playback, 3D Polaroid memories, hidden riddle gates, and warm paper animations tailored to one specific story.

No cloned templates. No disposable builders. We build digital heirlooms that recipients revisit long after the candles are blown out and the party music fades.

I Architecture

Bespoke By Design

Every keepsake is hand-coded from scratch. Typography, spatial rhythm, palette, and chapter structure are art-directed specifically around your story, never poured into a cookie-cutter container.

II Sensory

Tactile Intimacy

Physical stationery gestures brought to glass: wax seal medallions that break on touch, spinning vinyl discs with synchronized tracks, blowable birthday candles, and secret passcode gates.

III Longevity

Permanent & Private

Hosted on a dedicated private link (e.g. cupid.studio/ama24) with optional passcode gating. Ad-free, private, and preserved indefinitely—with matching printable cardstock QR gift tags.
